An airport Travelodge that serves its purpose as a transit stop. The room is modern and well-maintained with clean bed linen neatly turned down. There's an upholstered chair by the window with cushions for some comfort, and a safe under the nightstand which is practical for travelers.

The bathroom is compact with red accent tiles that give it a slightly dated feel despite being clean. White fixtures are shiny and spotless. It's small and clearly designed for function over comfort — typical for this tier.

The view from the room looks out onto the airport parking lot with planes and the control tower visible in the distance. Not scenic, but at least you know you're close to your terminal.

**Style:** Modern, functional  
**Condition:** Well-maintained  
**Luxury level:** Budget  
**Cleanliness:** Good throughout  
**Notable features:** In-room safe, airport proximity, upholstered chair  
**Drawbacks:** Compact bathroom, parking lot view, no amenities beyond basics
