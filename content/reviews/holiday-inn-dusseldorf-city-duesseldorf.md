A solid mid-range option on Düsseldorf's Toulouser Allee, the Holiday Inn delivers a modern and well-maintained room with some upscale touches. The room features contemporary furniture, stylish lighting, artwork on the walls, and an ergonomic desk setup by the window — practical for business travelers. The bathroom stands out with a large walk-in shower and a separate bathtub, which is a welcome addition at this price point.

Cleanliness was impeccable throughout. The window offers a decent view of the surrounding area with some greenery visible. The room feels well-organized with ample storage space.

On the downside, it's still a chain hotel and the design, while clean, lacks any real character or personality. The corridor and common areas have that typical Holiday Inn feel. Nothing memorable, but nothing to complain about either.

**Style:** Modern, corporate  
**Condition:** Well-maintained, everything in good shape  
**Luxury level:** Mid-range with some upscale touches (bathtub)  
**Cleanliness:** Excellent  
**Notable features:** Bathtub + separate shower, desk workspace, artwork  
**Drawbacks:** Generic chain hotel atmosphere, no distinctive character
