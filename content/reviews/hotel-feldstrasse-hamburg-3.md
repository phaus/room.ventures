A third visit to the Feldstraße hotel, this time in a suite configuration with a separate living area. The same modern aesthetic seen in the July 2016 visit continues — hardwood floors, neutral tones, the signature red wingback chair — but here there's a partition wall separating the bedroom from a small lounge with a grey sofa, coffee table with magazines, and a work desk. The bedroom, visible through the opening, features the same upholstered headboard and white linens. The bathroom is functional but unremarkable: a glass-enclosed walk-in shower, wall-mounted toilet, granite vanity with a single undermount sink, wall-mounted hairdryer, and modern light fixtures. The dark brown floor tiles and granite counter give the bathroom a slightly heavier feel than the airy bedroom. Overall a competent suite upgrade — the extra space is appreciated — but nothing about it rises above standard business hotel fare.
