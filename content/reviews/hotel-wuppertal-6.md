Sixth stay at this Holiday Inn Express next to Wuppertal Hauptbahnhof, and the formula holds: clean, compact, utterly impersonal. The room has modern, neutral furniture, a desk, and adequate lighting, but no storage to speak of and zero character. The bathroom is tiled with a glass shower enclosure and separate toilet — functional and clean.

The window view is the honest part of the deal: railway bridge, graffiti, a strip of greenery. Fine for one night if the train station location is the point, which it is.

Nothing broken, nothing dirty, nothing memorable. At this price the room does its job, but the chain has coasted on its location for years — no renovation energy, no upgrade since the earlier visits beyond patching the gaps.
