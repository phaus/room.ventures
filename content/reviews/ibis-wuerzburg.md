Standard ibis "new design" room on Veitshöchheimer Straße: large bed with crisp white linens, dark wood and metal accents, minimal furniture, neutral palette. Looks clean and well-kept, no visible wear — exactly what the chain template promises.

Big window is the strong point by day; by evening (both photos taken at 20:50) the room leans on dim ambient lighting and feels a bit dark. The real trade-off is sound: the hotel sits right next to the hospital, and street noise is hard to ignore with the window open. Closed-window situation all night — working A/C is what makes that survivable. Check it actually cools before accepting the room.

Room itself is fine for an ibis: functional, tidy, nothing beyond the standard. The location noise is what keeps this at a 3.
