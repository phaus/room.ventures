A typical Premier Inn experience in Tamworth. The room is functional and clean but the furniture design feels dated despite being in reasonable condition. Two nightstands flank the bed with several power outlets available for charging devices — one of Premier Inn's better practical touches.

The bathroom is modern with overhead and recessed ceiling lighting, but the floor shows some dark grey spots that could be grout stains or embedded dirt, suggesting cleaning could be more thorough. The second room photo reveals a more spacious area with decent ceiling height.

It's a budget chain hotel and it delivers exactly what you'd expect: a clean bed, a functional bathroom, and not much else. No frills, no surprises.

**Style:** Modern but dated furniture  
**Condition:** Dated, showing its age  
**Luxury level:** Budget  
**Cleanliness:** Decent, some floor staining in bathroom  
**Notable features:** Good power outlet placement, spacious for a budget hotel  
**Drawbacks:** Dated furniture design, bathroom floor stains, no character
