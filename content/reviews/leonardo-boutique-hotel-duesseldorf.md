Boutique-chain room on Oststraße in Düsseldorf's Stadtmitte, done in the predictable Leonardo design language: marble-patterned wall panel, modern art above the bed, brown throw over white linens, chaise lounge in the corner. Looks fresh — surfaces, carpet and bathroom all read recently done and very clean.

The bathroom is the strongest part: black-and-white tile scheme, large lit mirror, handheld shower with proper controls, extraction fan. Counter space next to the sink is tight, though.

The room photos make the honest trade-off visible: the layout is packed — chaise, desk table with lamp and room tablet, door to the bathroom all compete for space, and the whole thing feels a bit cramped and sparse despite the design effort. Minimal amenities, no character beyond the chain template. Good lighting and large windows help.

Solid 4 for the condition and the bathroom; the boutique label mostly covers good upkeep, not much else.
